# Tutorials

Each tutorial follows a study from start to finish with tidyvisor: the
question, the design and sample size, the data audit, the choice of
analysis and its assumptions, the interpretation, and a model paragraph
for the report.

| Tutorial | Study | What it shows |
|----|----|----|
| [A diet experiment, from plan to report](https://tidyvisor.in4metrix.dev/articles/tutorial-experiment.md) | Growth of chicks on four diets (`ChickWeight`; Crowder and Hand 1990) | Power analysis, pseudoreplication, a mixed model, the report |
| [Comparing treatments with counts](https://tidyvisor.in4metrix.dev/articles/tutorial-counts.md) | Insects on plots treated with six sprays (`InsectSprays`; Beall 1942) | Assumption checks, why ANOVA fails, a count model, the report |
